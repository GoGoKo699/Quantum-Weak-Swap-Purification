# Related work

The problem is universal purity amplification: turn repeated noisy copies of an unknown pure target into one physical output of higher fidelity.

- **Streaming strong-SWAP purification.** [Childs et al., *Streaming quantum state purification*](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.16387v4) recursively combine equally purified samples with the symmetric-projection SWAP test. Their protocol attains an expected sample cost proportional to inverse infidelity for fixed input noise, independently of dimension. Its recursive evaluation retains a logarithmic stack of intermediate states. They also give the unequal-input strong-projection formula in Appendix A. Here the retained state meets a fresh sample through a weaker coherent filter. This gives the same sample order with one stored data register in the specified noise regime.
- **Purification maps and circuit implementation.** [Yao et al., *Protocols and trade-offs of quantum state purification*](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.01138v3) study symmetric projections on equal-noise inputs, circuit constructions and recursive extensions with reduced depth. [He, Xiong and Wang, *A nonstabilizerness resource law for universal quantum state purification*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08626) give in Eq. (5) a weighted sum of separately projected symmetric and antisymmetric terms for identical copies. Such copies commute with SWAP, so the cross blocks vanish. In the streaming problem the copies acquire unequal purities. The [weak SWAP instrument](../science/weak-swap.md) preserves the cross blocks on success. Measuring the sector and accepting with different probabilities has a strict fidelity ceiling for this architecture.
- **Collective optimal purification.** [Cirac, Ekert and Macchiavello](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.82.4344) establish optimal collective purification of single qubits. [Li et al., *Optimal quantum purity amplification*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18167) characterize the collective problem in arbitrary dimension and demonstrate SWAPNET on a fixed three-input block. These results set the collective performance benchmark. The memory and renewal analysis here concerns a streaming receiver that permanently discards all but one quantum register after each round.
- **State estimation.** [Massar and Popescu](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.74.1259) give the pure-qubit estimation fidelity. [Bruß and Macchiavello](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0375-9601(99)00099-7) extend the analysis to arbitrary dimension. The [per-transcript benchmark](../science/measure-prepare.md) applies the pure-state moment identity to receivers that are free to choose their stopping times.
- **Stabilizer restrictions.** [Stabilizer-Purification-Thresholds](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Stabilizer-Purification-Thresholds) treats the first universal fidelity gain under stabilizer processing of noisy qubit copies. Here the joint SWAP instrument uses coherent control and targets arbitrary output accuracy under a persistent-memory constraint.
